# StegLearn Preview — Transaction Boundary

## Intake boundary
A bounded learning-loop pilot with a parent/steward owner, learner-appropriate activity scope, permitted evidence types, privacy/data-handling constraints and explicit review responsibility. Parent/steward review remains required for accepted learning evidence.

## Deliverables
A bounded Wonder → Build/Observe → Explain → Parent Review → Learning Receipt → Portfolio Record flow, applicable source lesson/material, structured receipt/portfolio evidence and a pilot findings report.

## Exclusions
No generally released education platform, legal homeschool-compliance automation, learner identity/destiny scoring, autonomous AI authority over the learner, authenticated external-provider mutation, or learner-private-data egress without separately admitted capability.

## Acceptance
Complete when the agreed learning loop produces a reviewable structured receipt and portfolio record and the parent/steward can accept, correct or decline the evidence.

## Commercial terms
Pilot fee, duration, support, content scope, privacy/retention terms and any external media/provider integration are separately agreed. No universal price is asserted.

## Evidence / receipt
Retain activity/source provenance, learner explanation, parent/steward review state, structured learning receipt and portfolio record according to the agreed privacy boundary.

## Unreleased substrate not sold
The current source/materialized learning flows are a development preview, not a generally released platform. Google/YouTube partnership capabilities, generalized curriculum generation and external authenticated integrations are not sold as already proven.
