# Governance Observatory Research / Evaluation Support — Transaction Boundary

## Intake boundary
Identify the external framework or observation question, authorized/public source set, requested observation/evaluation depth, publication/privacy constraints, and accountable customer owner.

## Deliverables
A bounded engagement may include source capture, claim/non-claim extraction, reproducible artifact hashing, observation/test records, comparison artifacts, publication-readiness findings, and a final evidence package.

## Exclusions
No endorsement, certification, execution authorization, legal/regulatory conclusion, partnership claim, authority transfer, or promise that an external framework is admissible.

## Acceptance
Complete when the agreed observation/evaluation artifacts are delivered with their source/provenance and publication boundaries and any unresolved source-dependent fields remain explicit.

## Commercial terms
Fee, schedule, source volume, publication rights, support and retention are separately quoted for the engagement. The released v0.1.0 toolkit does not itself establish a paid-service price.

## Evidence / receipt
Retain source capture records, artifact hashes, observation/test/result identifiers, publication state and customer delivery/acceptance evidence.

## Release boundary
Governance Observatory v0.1.0 is an immutable released baseline. Research/evaluation support does not imply that current main or later source changes are included in v0.1.0.
