# StegCRM Managed Pilot — Transaction Boundary

## Intake boundary
Select one bounded commercial workflow and identify its authoritative source systems, source-native records/stages, permitted read/export access, customer owner, service associations and evidence references. The pilot begins as read/reconciliation projection; source write-back requires a separately admitted transition.

## Deliverables
1. source-authority and relationship map;
2. governed relationship/opportunity projection;
3. source-native stage and factual interaction reconciliation;
4. evidence-reference register;
5. next-action projection where source evidence supports it;
6. pilot findings and reconciliation report.

## Exclusions
No replacement of the customer's CRM authority, no invented qualification/probability/revenue state, no automatic source write-back, no credential authority, and no claim that projection output itself changed a source system.

## Acceptance
Complete when the agreed source records are represented with their declared authority/evidence lineage, projection authority remains NONE, discrepancies are identified, and the customer receives the bounded reconciliation output.

## Commercial terms
Pilot price, duration, source count, data handling, retention, support and any later write-enabled integration are separately quoted in the written scope. No universal price is asserted.

## Evidence / receipt
Retain source coordinates/evidence references, projection identity, reconciliation state and delivery/acceptance evidence without copying private message bodies into public repositories.

## Unreleased substrate not sold
The managed pilot sells governed projection/reconciliation work. It does not sell a standalone released StegCRM SaaS/product, autonomous CRM authority, or an already-admitted write-back runtime.
