# ICV Governance Assessment — Transaction Boundary

## Customer outcome
A bounded, evidence-backed map of one declared workflow: authoritative actors and sources, decision boundaries, allowed/prohibited/conditional transitions, failure conditions, evidence gaps, and a governed target-state implementation plan.

## Intake prerequisites
Before engagement, identify the customer, one accountable customer owner, the bounded workflow, authorized source material, data-handling constraints, and the decisions or transitions to be assessed. Credentials, private keys, or unrelated regulated/private data are not required and must not be supplied merely for assessment.

## Included deliverables
1. agreed assessment boundary and source register;
2. current-state authority/transition map;
3. evidence and ambiguity register;
4. fail-closed and escalation findings;
5. target-state governance plan;
6. final findings review and bounded disposition.

## Exclusions
This assessment does not grant execution, credential, legal, regulatory, release, provider, payment, custody, or customer-system authority. It is not certification and does not represent that a proposed runtime is deployed.

## Commercial terms
Price, schedule, retention, intellectual-property terms, and any follow-on implementation are quoted in the written scope for the specific engagement. No universal numeric price is asserted by this package.

## Acceptance
The assessment is accepted when the agreed deliverables are delivered and the customer either confirms receipt/acceptance or supplies one consolidated factual-correction list within the written review period. Acceptance does not convert findings into operational authorization.

## Evidence boundary
The engagement records the sources evaluated and the basis for each finding. Unsupported assumptions remain unresolved rather than being promoted into customer or StegVerse authority.
