# Governance & AI Boundary Validation — Transaction Boundary

## Intake boundary
One bounded AI/automation workflow, one accountable human owner, one governance-manifest v1 artifact, accurate authority declarations, and an approved data-handling plan. Credentials, private keys, production secrets, regulated personal data, and export-controlled material are excluded unless a separately authorized handling agreement exists.

## Deliverables
One deterministic manifest validation, machine-readable findings, human-readable audit report, findings-review session, one corrected-manifest validation cycle, reproduction coordinates, and a final pilot disposition.

## Exclusions
No runtime monitoring/enforcement, production authorization, legal/regulatory/security certification, penetration testing, custody, payments, trading, identity issuance, token activity, or undeclared-system evaluation.

## Acceptance
Complete when the recorded manifest, validator version and validation time reproduce the agreed artifacts; required findings are explained; and no output implies production approval or certification.

## Commercial terms
Fixed-scope paid assessment or research collaboration. Price, IP, publication rights, support, travel, retention and follow-on licensing require the engagement-specific written agreement.

## Evidence / receipt
Retain manifest identity, validator version, validation time, findings artifacts, reproduction information and final disposition.

## Unreleased substrate not sold
The validator's pilot service is being sold, not a released production enforcement runtime or hosted control plane. No product VERSION, Git tag or GitHub Release is implied.
